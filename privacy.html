Hex Merge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Hex Merge (“the App”) is a puzzle game published by Dhaval Patel, available as an iPhone/iPad app and as a web version at dhpa69.github.io/hex-merge. This policy explains what information the App handles. We do not sell your data, and the App contains no analytics SDK of our own.

Guest play (default)

No account is needed. Your display name, coins, items (skins, themes, effects, power-ups), XP and level, chests, badges, missions, stats and settings are saved on your device (local storage) and do not leave it except as described below.

Optional Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in, Apple shares a unique account identifier and, only if you allow it, your name and email (which may be a private relay address). We store your game profile (the items listed above) in our database, hosted by Supabase, only to sync your progress across your devices. The first time you sign in on a device, that device's guest progress is added to your account.

Deleting your account

In the App go to Home → 👤 Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your Hex Merge cloud profile and your sign-in (if you also use our Star Tiles game with the same Apple ID, your Star Tiles profile is kept and only the Hex Merge data is removed). Progress on the device stays as a guest. You can also email us to request deletion, including of leaderboard entries.

Leaderboards

When you finish a game against the computer, a Daily Puzzle, a team game or an online game, your score, display name, mode/difficulty and a random per-install device id may be submitted to public leaderboards. Other players can see your display name and score.

Online play and chat

Online games are private rooms joined with a room code or invite link. Moves, chat messages you type, your display name, your online rank and a random session id are relayed through a public MQTT-over-WebSocket relay so the other devices can sync. We do not log these messages. Do not share personal information in chat; you can turn chat off at any time in Home → More.

Advertising and tracking (iPhone/iPad app)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the home and results screens, a full-screen ad between some games, and optional rewarded videos (for coins or an extra chest). AdMob may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are non-personalized. You can change this in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See How Google uses information from apps. The web version shows no ads.

Coins and chests

Coins, chests and items are earned in the game and have no cash value. There are no real-money purchases in this version.

Sharing

The Share button opens your device's share sheet with your score and an invite link. Nothing is posted unless you choose to.

Service providers

Supabase (database and sign-in), Apple (sign-in), Google AdMob (ads, app only), GitHub Pages (hosting), and the public online-play relay.

Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
