👥 Team 2v2

Two teams of two on a bigger board (61 hexes). Teammates share a score. 10 turns each (a turn = all 3 tray pieces), turns go around the table.

The computers use the difficulty picked on the home screen.
❓ How to play

Your turn: place ALL 3 of your tray pieces, in any order — tap a piece, then tap an empty hex. Merges happen after each drop. Once all 3 are placed your tray refills and the turn passes. Your tray never has 3 identical pieces. The game ends after everyone's 10 turns, or as soon as the board is full.

★ Wild piece (rare rainbow hex, at most 1 per tray): when placed it turns into whichever neighbor number gives the best merge. With no neighbors it becomes a 1.

Merge: when 3 or more hexes with the same number touch, they merge into ONE hex of the next number, right where you dropped (three 2s → a 3).

Score: number × 10 × hexes merged. If the new hex makes another group of 3+, it merges again: the 2nd merge scores ×2, the 3rd ×3 (combo!).

Supernova: merging up to a 9 blasts it and its neighbors for a 500+ bonus.

⚡ Power-ups (tap the ⚡ button; the badge shows how many free ones you have — 2 per game, then 50 coins each; 1 per turn, each type once per game): ✖2 Double makes ALL 3 drops of your turn score double (arm it before your 1st drop — tap it again to cancel for a refund), 💣 Bomb clears a hex and its 6 neighbors, ✚ Cross Blast clears the 3 lines through a hex. Blasts score nothing. ⇄ Swap (its own button) replaces the pieces left in your tray: 1 per turn, 1 free per game, then 50 coins — tap twice to confirm a paid swap.

Coins: start with 100, win = +10, plus win-streak, mission and daily login bonuses.
