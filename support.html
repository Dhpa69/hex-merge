How to play

Each turn, place all 3 pieces from your tray on empty hexes. Three or more touching hexes with the same number merge into the next number; merges can chain into combos. Merge up to 9 for a supernova. The rainbow ★ wild piece becomes whichever number gives the best merge. The game ends after 10 turns each, or when the board is full. Tap ❓ How to on the home screen for the full rules.

Common questions

My coins or items are gone. Progress is saved on the device. To keep it across devices or after reinstalling, sign in with Apple (Home → 👤 Account).

How do I play with a friend online? Tap “Host online game”, share the room code, and your friend enters it under “Join”.

Can I turn off chat? Yes — Home → ⚙️ More → Chat: OFF.

Ads. The app shows a small banner on menus, an occasional ad between games, and optional videos for rewards. No ads appear while you're placing pieces.
